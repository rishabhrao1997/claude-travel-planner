I've planned the day so you only use two stations, Osaka and Kyoto. Both are big stations with lifts. In Kyoto, taxis take you door to door, so there are no small stations and no hill climbs. Taxi rides also count as sit-downs.

**Big caution:** mid-to-late November is peak autumn-leaf season. Taxi queues at Kyoto Station can be long and traffic is slow. Book a **sightseeing taxi** that stays with you for about 5 hours (MK Taxi or Yasaka Taxi). Then the driver waits at each stop and you never queue. Price and booking lead time: **CHECK**.

## Plan

| Time | Stop | Longest walk | Lift exit / drop-off | Sit-down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8:45 | **JR Osaka Stn** → Special Rapid to Kyoto (~30 min) | 5 min (gate to platform) | **Central Gate (中央口)**, lift to platform. Lift location: **CHECK** | Train seat. Seats aren't reserved, so book an **A-Seat** (paid reserved seat). Times and price: **CHECK** |

| 9:50 | **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion) | ~5 min each way (drop-off → gate → pond view), gentle slope | Taxi to the main entrance | Benches near pond: **CHECK**. Free wheelchairs at entrance, no booking needed |
| 10:45 | Taxi → **Arashiyama** | – | Drop at Togetsukyo Bridge | Taxi, 30 min |
| 11:15 | **Early lunch** by Togetsukyo, e.g. Arashiyama Yoshimura (soba) | – | Ask for **table seats, not tatami**. Lift/stairs: **CHECK** | Lunch, ~1 hr |
| 12:30 | **Tenryu-ji** garden | ~5 min (restaurant → main gate), ~5 min (gate → pond garden) | Walk from lunch | Sit on the main hall veranda facing the pond. Shoes off, one step up: **CHECK** |
| 13:30 | *Optional:* **Bamboo grove**, 5 min in, then turn back | 5 min | Way out via Tenryu-ji North Gate: **CHECK** for steps. Skip it if they're tired | – |
| 13:45 | Taxi → **Kyoto Stn** | – | Drop at Central Exit | Taxi, 30–45 min |
| 14:30 | Tea in the station building, then train home before the 17:00 rush | 5 min (taxi → café → platform) | Central Exit, lift to platform | Café, then train seat (A-Seat again) |

## Rules check
- **Walking:** the longest stretch is about 5 minutes.
- **Lifts:** only Osaka and Kyoto stations are used. Both are major JR stations. Confirm the exact lift on the day: **CHECK**.
- **Sit-downs:** never more than about 1 hour without one.

## Things to know
- **Kinkaku-ji:** the temple says that past the pavilion **there are steps**. People with weak legs should **turn back the way they came** at the pavilion rather than finish the loop.
- **Tenryu-ji** closes at 17:00 from late October to late March. Its accessible toilet is inside the grounds. At the gate, ask staff for the step-free route.
- **Fallen leaves** make paths slippery in November. Bring cash for temple fees.
- **Staying near Shin-Osaka instead?** Take the Shinkansen to Kyoto (~15 min). A reserved seat means a guaranteed seat.
